The job

What a bouncer at Octagon Defense does

You'll be the calm person in a loud room. Most of the job is paying attention and talking to people. Getting hands-on is the last resort.

Octagon Defense is hiring part-time bouncers in Lubbock, Texas. Pay is $15–20 an hour, based on experience. Bouncers work the door and the floor at bars, event venues, house parties, student events and private celebrations, with a de-escalation-first approach.

What you'll do

  • Run the door: check guest lists or IDs and keep out people who shouldn't be there.
  • Work the floor: stay visible, watch the crowd and spot trouble early.
  • Calm situations down before they turn physical.
  • Walk guests out calmly when they need to leave.
  • Help clear the space and keep the exit orderly at the end of the night.

Who we're looking for

  • People who stay calm when a room gets loud.
  • Good talkers who would rather settle a problem than start one.
  • Reliable people who show up on time and stay until the job is done.
  • Door, event, combat sports, wrestling or martial arts experience helps.
