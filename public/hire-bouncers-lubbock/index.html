The job

What our bouncers do at your event

A bouncer from Octagon Defense controls the door, watches the room and handles problems early, so the host, the manager and the bar staff can do their jobs.

  • Run the door

    Check the guest list or IDs, keep a count, and keep out people who shouldn't be there.

  • Work the floor

    Stay visible, watch the crowd and spot trouble before it starts.

  • Calm things down

    Step in early when a guest gets loud, a disagreement heats up or someone has had too much.

  • Walk people out

    When someone needs to leave, we get them out calmly and without a scene.

  • Close out the night

    Help clear the space at the end and keep the exit orderly.

Planning

How many bouncers do you need?

A common starting point is one bouncer for every 100 guests, or one for every 50 when alcohol is served. Add one more for each extra entrance you want watched.

Treat the table as a starting point. A packed bar, a big backyard with two gates, or a crowd that's been drinking since noon can all need more. Tell us about your event and we'll recommend a number.
