The owner

Meet Brennan Thomasson

Brennan Thomasson owns and runs Octagon Defense. He graduated from Texas Tech University in Lubbock, where the company is based and where most of its work is today.

His background is in MMA and wrestling. Combat sports teach the things that matter most at a crowded door: stay calm under pressure, read people early, and stay in control without losing your temper.

As owner and operator, Brennan is the person you talk to when you book Octagon Defense, and the person responsible for how the night goes.

The name

Why the octagon

An octagon has eight sides. In MMA, it's the cage where fighters learn to stay composed when things get intense.

Both ideas fit the work. Cover every side of the room, and stay calm when it gets tense.

How we work

What Octagon Defense stands for

We call what we do protection, because the job is keeping people safe and the event running, not looking tough at the door.

  • Talk before anything else

    Most problems end with a calm conversation. Physical force is the last resort.

  • Notice trouble early

    Watching the room closely means fewer problems ever reach the point of needing a bouncer.

  • Own the result

    The owner is on the phone when you book and answerable for how the night goes.
